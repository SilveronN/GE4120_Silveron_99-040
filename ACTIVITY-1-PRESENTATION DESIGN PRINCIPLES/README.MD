# 🟡Instruction:
### We were tasked to make a poster for free coffee event with a tagline, with the ADDU Department of Nursing as the author. The poster contained the date as well as the location of the event.

# 🟠Reflection:
### I learned that a good poster has to communicate the important details clearly, like the date, the location, and a catchy tagline that makes people want to come. Creating it for the ADDU Department of Nursing taught me to think about my audience and to keep the design simple and easy to read. It also showed me how much layout, colors, and wording affect how well a message gets across.
